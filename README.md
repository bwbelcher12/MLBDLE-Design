# MLBDLE
**Novel MLB Daily Game**

# About
MLBDLE is a daily game in the same vein as [Wordle](https://www.nytimes.com/games/wordle/index.html), [Scrandle](https://scrandle.com/), or [Nerdle](https://nerdlegame.com/). Each day, players can attempt to guess the official lineup card for their favorite team, or any team that is playing a game that day. Using real-time MLB API data, published lineups are reflected on the website the moment they become available, letting players make their guesses up to the last moment. Share your scores with others and maintain a per-team streak! Historical lineup cards from baseball history will give players a challenge when their team isn't playing and will give everyone something to do during the offseason.
